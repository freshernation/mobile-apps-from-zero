# Day 3 — Checkpoint

> **By the end of today** your third screen is underway, and you and your instructor
> have agreed what Friday's demo is, cut to fit if needed.

## The checkpoint (15 minutes, with your instructor)

Bring:
- your app running on your phone;
- `CAPSTONE.md`, with what's done ticked;
- `bash tool/check.sh` output.

You'll answer three questions:

1. **What works end to end right now?** Show it.
2. **What's left for the demo?** In slices, with a guess for each.
3. **What's the riskiest thing left?** The one you're least sure you can finish.

Then you decide together: **keep, cut, or swap.** A cut isn't a failure. Cutting to
finish is the most professional thing a developer does. A three-screen app that works,
with tests, beats a five-screen app that half-works, every time, including on the
rubric.

Write the result into `CAPSTONE.md` under **Checkpoint**: what's in, what's cut.

## After the checkpoint

Finish your third slice the day 2 way. By the end of today, every screen on Friday's
demo should **exist and work**, even if it's plain. Tomorrow is for making it good,
not for making it exist.
